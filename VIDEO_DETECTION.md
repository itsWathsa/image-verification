# Video screening and evaluation

## What the app detects

The app applies its frozen Detectra-OpenFake image model to up to 12 uniformly spaced frames. The visible average is a mean of model scores, not an AI-generated-video probability. A frame scoring at least 0.90 produces an AI-like-frame indication; uniformly low scores do not establish camera origin. There is no audio, optical-flow, lip-sync or learned temporal analysis.

A video depicting robots or artificial intelligence is not automatically generated by AI. Search categories and filenames are not ground-truth labels. The supplied `7688618-uhd_2160_4096_24fps.mp4` matches a [Pexels listing](https://www.pexels.com/video/robotic-hand-in-front-of-vintage-tv-7688618/) credited to Yaroslav Shuraev, dated 28 April 2021, at 2160 x 4096 and 24 fps. This listing does not verify AI generation. The clip is excluded from positive training labels and benchmark metrics. Its low score cannot be called a confirmed false negative on the available evidence.

## Dataset

[DeepAction v1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/faridlab/deepaction_v1) by Matyas Bohacek and Hany Farid contains documented generated human-action videos from AnimateDiff, CogVideoX5B, RunwayML, Stable Diffusion, Veo and VideoPoet, alongside matched Pexels real videos. We pin revision `476932c3369424ca59dc1f8fb758cb3c13fe63fe`.

Generated videos use CC BY 4.0; the Pexels videos retain the Pexels license. The utility downloads media for local evaluation; it does not redistribute clips, execute dataset code or upload user media. Please cite:

> Matyas Bohacek and Hany Farid. Human Action CLIPS: Detecting AI-generated Human Motion. 2024. arXiv:2412.00526.

## Bounded evaluation protocol

`prepare_video_benchmark.py` selects 32 real action families from files no larger than 20,000,000 bytes, shuffles their sorted paths with seed 20261120, and takes one clip from each of two generator sources for each family. Generator sources rotate through the six sorted names. Each source/family uses its first sorted file. Total: 32 real and 64 generated clips, about 459 MB. The first 16 selected families are development data and the other 16 are test data. Matching action groups never cross splits. File sizes and available published LFS SHA-256 hashes are checked after download. Local SHA-256 hashes for every selected file are recorded in the manifest.

This is a small, size-filtered convenience subset, not the canonical DeepAction benchmark or a claim about modern generators. The subset can contain other source biases; prompt grouping does not prove the absence of all near-duplicate content. The underlying image model's full training-data overlap is unknown. Real controls come from one source, and generated videos are from older models. Generator-held-out and independent modern camera/video sets are still required before replacing the detector.

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -B prepare_video_benchmark.py
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -B evaluate_video.py .models/video-evaluation/manifest.json --output .models/video-evaluation/frame-screen-v2
```

The evaluation utility rejects duplicate paths, duplicate content, invalid labels and action-family leakage. It reports missed generated clips, false alarms on real clips, recall, false-positive rate, per-source counts and decoding errors. Unavailable analyses are reported separately; they are not treated as correct classifications. Reports and downloaded clips remain under ignored `.models/`. No detector weights are retrained.

An offline alternative requires at least one quarter of sampled frames (at least two frames) to score 0.50 or above, instead of accepting any frame at 0.90. It is a fixed comparison rule, not a trained temporal model. Its development gate requires at least a 10 percentage-point recall gain, a false-positive rate no higher than 5%, and no increase in false alarms. A small subset alone cannot establish deployment reliability; the candidate remains disabled in the app.

## Dataset-driven decoding fix

Some Veo dataset files overstate their frame counts. In one checked clip, the header reports 194 frames but only indices through 191 decode. Previously, requesting frame 193 made the entire analysis unavailable. Sampling version 2 retries at most the two preceding final frames. Reports retain both requested and sampled frame indices, accurate corresponding timestamps, and a count of sampling adjustments. It does not substitute distant content, duplicate an already sampled frame or conceal unresolved decode failures. Other sampling failures still produce an unavailable assessment.

## Recorded pilot results

The 20 November 2026 run completed all 96 selected videos, with no unavailable analyses. The dataset cache passed the preparation utility's integrity checks. Ten Veo clips required the bounded endpoint retry. Aggregates are retained in [VIDEO_DETECTION_RESULTS.json](VIDEO_DETECTION_RESULTS.json); per-video and frame-level reports remain in the local ignored cache.

| Split and rule | Generated flagged | Generated missed | Real falsely flagged | Real false-alarm rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Development: current any-frame 0.90 | 32 / 32 | 0 | 6 / 16 | 37.5% |
| Test: current any-frame 0.90 | 32 / 32 | 0 | 4 / 16 | 25.0% |
| Development: offline consensus 0.50 | 30 / 32 | 2 | 4 / 16 | 25.0% |
| Test: offline consensus 0.50 | 32 / 32 | 0 | 4 / 16 | 25.0% |

The alternative failed its development gate and is not enabled. No model was trained or fine-tuned. The endpoint retry improves decoding compatibility; these results do not establish improved classification accuracy. Catching every generated clip in this small, older-generator subset does not imply 100% general recall. The measured real-video false alarms remain too frequent for a definitive AI-generated-versus-real verdict. The application retains explicitly experimental frame screening and its visible score, with a warning about false alarms.

A replacement needs evaluation against independent modern generators, diverse real camera footage, conventional edits and compression changes, with source- and generator-disjoint holdouts. No source-specific exception or target-video hash is used by the app's scoring code.
